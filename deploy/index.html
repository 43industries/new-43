FAQ.

Answers to what people ask us most
What is 43 Industries?
+
43 Industries is a Nairobi-based wealth company operating across three divisions: Fintech (payment rails & investment infrastructure), Digital Assets (BTC, ETH, SOL, TRX, XRP multi-chain operations), and Metals & Markets — physical precious metals (gold, silver, copper) with vault custody, plus access to listed stocks and ETFs for diversified public-market exposure. We also run an online shop selling tech, clothing, hardware, and more. Securities services are offered only where permitted and with appropriate disclosures; nothing on this site is investment advice. Operationally, fintech telemetry and compliance hooks feed the digital-asset settlement layer; vault, tokenization, and broker hand-offs in Metals & Markets reconcile back through the same audit and reporting fabric.
How do stocks & ETFs work here?
+
Our Metals & Markets division pairs LBMA bullion with listed equities and ETFs — from broad index and sector funds to single-name exposure you choose with guidance from our team. Execution may be on-platform or through licensed partners depending on your jurisdiction. Contact us with your goals and we will outline onboarding and documentation. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
How do I buy crypto?
+
Visit the shop section and select any crypto_assets product. Add to cart, proceed to checkout, and choose CRYPTO as your payment method. You'll receive a wallet address and instructions after order confirmation. For larger institutional purchases, contact us directly at partners@43industries.com.
Does the shop ship worldwide?
+
Yes — we ship physical products (phones, computers, clothes, hardware, precious metals) to over 50 countries. Digital products (software, crypto) are delivered instantly after payment confirmation. Shipping times vary by region: 3-5 days for East Africa, 7-14 days internationally. All precious metal shipments are fully insured.
Which metals are available?
+
We offer LBMA-certified gold bars (1oz, 10oz, 1kg), .999 fine silver bars and coins, and investment-grade copper bars. All metals are sourced from certified refineries, come with assay certificates, and can be stored in our partner vault or shipped directly to you with full insurance.
How do I become a partner?
+
Fill out the contact form below, selecting your division of interest. For institutional or high-value partnerships, email partners@43industries.com directly. We respond within 24 hours and typically schedule a discovery call within 48 hours. All partnership inquiries are treated with strict confidentiality.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept Crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, TRX, XRP), card payments (Visa, Mastercard), and M-Pesa for East African customers. All transactions are encrypted and processed securely. Crypto payments settle instantly. Card and M-Pesa payments are confirmed within minutes.

What Fintech is

        
Fast, affordable rails for East Africa — and the foundation of 43 Industries.

        
43 Fintech moves value the way modern businesses expect: clear fees, short settlement times, and payout into the wallets people actually use — M-Pesa, bank accounts, and partner mobile-money networks. We design corridors around diaspora and regional trade, not around legacy wire habits.

        
Inside the company, Fintech is the shared money layer. When Digital Assets settle on-chain or Metals & Markets book bullion and equities, fiat legs, fees, and audit trails reconcile through this division. Outside the company, we partner with PSPs, SACCOs, MFIs, and product teams who need the same reliability without enterprise lock-in.

05
Layer 1 · rails_ingress
Last-mile money-in and money-out: mobile money, bank files, cards, and partner switches. Normalized into internal payment intents.
  • mobile_money — M-Pesa, Airtel, MTN adapters
  • bank_rails — ACH-style / RTGS hand-offs
  • acceptance — cards & checkout orchestration

        
06
Layer 2 · core_platform
API gateway, ledger, and event fabric. Idempotent commands, balances, and settlement state shared with Crypto and Metals reporting.
  • api_gateway — auth, rate limits, versioning
  • ledger — double-entry money records
  • events — webhooks & async bus

        
07
Layer 3 · compliance_plane
KYC, AML, sanctions, and transaction monitoring. Feeds the risk controls used by Digital Assets and Metals & Markets onboarding.
  • kyc_aml — identity & screening
  • tm_sars — monitoring & escalation paths
  • policy_engine — jurisdiction rules

        
08
Layer 4 · observability
SLA dashboards, structured audit logs, and alerting. Evidence packs for partners, auditors, and regulators.
  • audit_log — tamper-evident trails
  • slos — uptime & latency budgets
  • runbooks — incident & failover playbooks

Metals & markets — vault example

        

          
from industries43.metals import PreciousMetals

          
 

          
vault = PreciousMetals(account_id="YOUR_ID")

          
 

          
# Buy 1oz gold — physical + on-chain token

          
order = vault.buy(metal="GOLD", weight_oz=1, storage="vault")

          
 

          
# Add ETF sleeve (e.g. broad index) alongside bullion

          
sleeve = vault.markets.allocate_etf(ticker="CORE", pct=0.15)

          
 

          
print(order.token_id)    # → "GOLD_TOKEN_0x4f3..."

          
print(order.cert_url)    # → LBMA certificate link

          
print(order.vault_ref)    # → "NBO-VAULT-2024-1834"
